# 🧰 Prerequisites for the Hackathon

To make the most of your time and enjoy the vibe during the workshop, please prepare your tools and setup **before the event begins**.

---

## 💬 Use the Coding Assistant of Your Choice

You’ll be working with an AI coding assistant such as:

- ChatGPT (browser-based or app)
- Firebase Studio (browser-based)
- Cursor (IDE)
- Claude Code (CLI)
- Gemini (CLI, browser-based, or app)
- Or another tool you prefer

Make sure your **free plan or subscription** gives you access to enough tokens for at least **2 hours of active coding**.

---

## 🧑‍💻 IDE, Terminal & Copy-Paste

You can:
- Use a full IDE (like Cursor or Windsurf)
- Work directly in a terminal or browser-based environment
- Copy snippets from your assistant to your project

Choose whatever combination of tools works best for you.

---

## ⚠️ Prepare Tools in Advance

Public Wi-Fi at the hackathon may be limited.  
**Avoid wasting time by downloading tools upfront.** We recommend:

- Pre-install your IDE (e.g. **Cursor**, **Windsurf**, **VSCode** with **Copilot Agent**)
- Log into your assistant platform
- Confirm that your token quota or premium tier is active

This ensures smooth sailing once the coding begins.

---

## 🌐 Optional: Firebase CLI for Web Deployment

To submit your app, you’ll need to share one of the following on social media:
– A live demo link, OR
– A demo video

We recommend using **Firebase Hosting** for quickly deploying your Flutter web app to the web.

If you choose this option:
- Set up a new Firebase project
- Install the **Firebase CLI**  
  👉 https://firebase.google.com/docs/cli

✅ This step is **optional** — skip it if you plan to:
- Submit a video instead, **or**
- Use another hosting solution of your choice (e.g. GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify, etc.)

---

## 🧘 Come Prepared to Enjoy the Vibes

**We will reveal the target app during the workshop!**

🚫 You **do not** need to write any code in advance. Setting up an empty Firebase project is allowed.

✅ Just make sure your environment is ready, so you can dive straight into building the app and enjoying the creative energy.
